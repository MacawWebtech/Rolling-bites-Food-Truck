Meet The Founder

Rohan Mehta

"I wanted people to taste the same care in a five-minute burger that they'd expect from a five-course meal."

Rohan started Rolling Bites out of a converted delivery van with a single grill. Eight years later, the truck is a local institution — but the recipe hasn't changed: fresh, fast, and made with heart.
