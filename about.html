Our Story

How It Started

One Grill. One Idea.

In 2018, Rolling Bites hit the streets with a single converted van, one grill, and a menu of five items. The idea was simple: bring restaurant-quality street food to people wherever they were — no reservations, no pretense, just bold flavor served fast.

Today, we've grown into a full mobile kitchen with a rotating specials board, a catering arm, and a loyal following that tracks our location every single week.

Founder & Chef

Rohan Mehta

"Street food deserves the same craft as fine dining — just faster, and with better music."

A trained chef turned street-food evangelist, Rohan built Rolling Bites on the belief that the best meals are the ones shared standing up, napkin in hand, at the corner of a busy street.
